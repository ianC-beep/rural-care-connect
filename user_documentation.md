## Introduction

Promptmed Clinic Hub is a unified management platform designed for rural healthcare facilities. It integrates real-time supply chain tracking with patient triage queues, minimizing administrative overhead and allowing more resources to be directed toward patient care.

### Key Benefits

- **Real-time Inventory Tracking:** Monitor medical supply levels and receive automated alerts for low stock.
- **Dynamic Triage Dashboard:** Manage patient flow and prioritize critical cases.
- **Inter-Facility Logistics:** Request and transfer supplies between nearby clinics.
- **Specialist Telehealth:** Connect with urban specialists for remote consultations.

## Getting Started

### System Requirements

- **Device:** Tablet or desktop computer
- **Browser:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest versions)

1. Navigate to the Clinic Hub URL provided by your administrator.
2. Enter your credentials (username and password).

## The Nurse Beatrice Workflow

This section walks you through a typical day using the Clinic Hub, following Nurse Beatrice at a remote clinic.

### Step 1: Patient Entry & Triage

Nurse Beatrice registers a patient with severe symptoms using the mobile tablet, capturing vital data immediately.

- Tap the **New Patient** button on the dashboard.
- Enter patient details (name, age, symptoms).
- Use voice-to-text for quick symptom entry if preferred.
- The system automatically assigns a triage priority level.

### Step 2: Resource Check & Alerts

The Hub automatically flags low oxygen levels and high demand, alerting the regional administrator for urgent replenishment.

- Check the **Inventory Alerts** panel on the dashboard.
- Yellow alerts indicate supplies running low.
- Red alerts require immediate action.

### Step 3: Specialist Connection

Nurse Beatrice initiates a virtual consultation with an urban specialist to review the complex triage data.

- Select the patient from the triage queue.
- Click **Request Specialist Review**.
- Choose the appropriate specialty (e.g., Cardiology, General Medicine).
- The system notifies available specialists.

### Step 4: Coordinated Care Guidance

The specialist provides immediate guidance through the Hub, ensuring the patient receives the best possible care remotely.

- Accept the incoming video consultation request.
- Share patient vitals and lab results through the integrated viewer.
- Follow the specialist's recommendations and document them in the patient record.

### Step 5: Supply Replenishment

The supply chain system triggers an automated shipment, ensuring the clinic is prepared for the next wave of patients.

- Navigate to the **Supply Chain** view.
- Review pending transfer requests.
- Confirm receipt of incoming supplies.
- Update inventory levels upon delivery.

## Using the Clinic Hub Dashboard

### Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard provides a bird's-eye view of clinic operations:

- **Patient Queue:** Shows current patients waiting, ordered by triage priority.
- **Inventory Status:** Displays stock levels for critical medical supplies.
- **Alerts Panel:** Highlights urgent issues requiring attention.
- **Quick Actions:** Shortcuts to common tasks (new patient, request supplies, contact specialist).

### Navigation

- Use the sidebar menu to switch between different modules.
- Click on any alert to view detailed information.
- Use the search bar to find specific patients or supplies.

## Inventory Management

### Viewing Stock Levels

1. Navigate to the **Inventory** tab.
2. Supplies are color-coded:
   - **Green:** Adequate stock
   - **Yellow:** Running low (reorder soon)
   - **Red:** Critical shortage (immediate action required)

### Requesting Supplies

1. Click on a low-stock item.
2. Select **Request Replenishment**.
3. Choose the quantity and preferred supplier.
4. Submit the request. The system will track the shipment status.

### Inter-Facility Transfers

If a nearby facility has excess stock:

1. Go to **Supply Chain > Transfer Requests**.
2. Select the source facility and items needed.
3. Initiate the transfer. Both facilities will be notified.

## Specialist Referrals

### Initiating a Referral

1. Select the patient from the triage queue.
2. Click **Request Specialist Review**.
3. Attach relevant data (vitals, lab results, images).
4. Choose the specialty and urgency level.
5. Submit the referral.

### During a Consultation

- Use the integrated video call feature.
- Share patient records securely through the platform.
- Document the specialist's recommendations in the patient file.

## Interpreting Dashboard Results

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Description | Target |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| Inventory Accuracy | Percentage of stock levels correctly tracked | 100% |
| Task Completion Rate | Percentage of tasks completed successfully | >80% |
| User Confidence | Overall user satisfaction rating | High |

### Understanding Alerts

- **Yellow Alert:** Supply levels are below the reorder threshold. Plan to request replenishment within 48 hours.
- **Red Alert:** Critical shortage. Immediate action required to prevent stockout.
- **Blue Alert:** Informational update (e.g., incoming shipment confirmed).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What happens if I lose internet connection?**  
A: The Clinic Hub has offline-first capabilities. Data entered offline will sync automatically once connectivity is restored.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: Contact your clinic administrator or use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.

**Q: Can I use the app on my phone?**  
A: The Clinic Hub is optimized for tablets and desktops. A mobile-optimized version is planned for future releases.

**Q: Who do I contact for technical support?**  
A: See the Support Documentation for contact information and troubleshooting guides.
